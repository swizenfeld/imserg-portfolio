## Objective and context

As Lead Designer, I guided the end-to-end redesign of ViewSonic's global website to modernize its digital presence, improve scalability, and create a unified experience across all regions. The initiative focused on replatforming the site to Magento, implementing a CMS-driven, templated framework, and introducing a modern, responsive design system aligned with ViewSonic's premium brand and e-commerce goals.

The objective was to build a flexible, performance-driven platform that empowered both customers and internal teams, streamlining product discovery, global marketing campaigns, and content management.

## Design challenges

### Scalable framework

The previous site architecture made it difficult to maintain design consistency and roll out updates globally. The challenge was to design a modular, component-based system that could scale with the brand and let teams create pages through the CMS without developer dependency.

### Modernization

ViewSonic's online presence hadn't kept pace with its product lineup. The redesign needed a tech-forward, professional look that resonated with both consumer and enterprise audiences.

### Content flexibility

Marketing teams needed a faster workflow for launching campaigns. The solution centered on CMS-integrated templates and components, giving authors autonomy while preserving the design system's integrity.

### Performance and accessibility

Legacy templates caused slow load times and accessibility gaps. Improving Core Web Vitals, mobile performance, and WCAG 2.1 AA compliance was crucial to delivering a fast, inclusive experience.

## My approach

### Discovery and research

Partnered with marketing, engineering, and product teams to audit the current site, define user needs, and align on business goals. Competitive analysis informed benchmarks for design quality, navigation patterns, and performance.

### Design system

Developed a unified design system in Figma with reusable components, grid structures, and color tokens. The system supported multiple regions and kept campaigns and product launches consistent.

### Experience architecture

Redefined the information hierarchy, streamlined global navigation, and improved the content taxonomy to make product discovery intuitive across categories, solutions, and industries.

### Collaboration and integration

Worked closely with engineers to translate the design system into Magento's front-end architecture. Partnered with CMS developers to define component behavior and authoring guidelines for non-technical users.
