## Objective and context

Experian Boost enables millions of users to instantly raise their FICO® Scores by adding verified payment histories from utilities, telecom, streaming services, and more. As Senior Product Design Manager, my objective was to enhance the platform by expanding eligible payment categories, refining the user experience, and delivering financial literacy content to help consumers improve their credit scores.

## Design challenges

### Security

Streamlining complex interactions around securely linking financial accounts. Ensuring the process is intuitive and secure without sacrificing speed or user trust.

### Education

Providing accessible financial literacy content without overwhelming users. Balancing comprehensive information with simplicity to maintain engagement and prevent information overload.

### Technical limitations

Navigating technical limitations related to data integration, and working within existing constraints while striving to deliver a seamless experience.

### Integration

Ensuring seamless platform integration, intuitive navigation, and cohesive cross-feature interactions. Creating transparent, easily understood experiences that fit naturally into existing user flows.

## My approach

### User research and insights

- Analyzed feedback to pinpoint pain points in linking accounts and confusion around eligible payments.
- Discovered demand for additional categories, such as audio streaming and rent payments.

### Expanding payment categories

- Collaborated with the product team to identify and prioritize new eligible payment sources.
- Designed visual cues and straightforward messaging to highlight the expanded payment options.

### Refining the experience

- Simplified onboarding, reducing friction and drop-off during account linking.
- Added personalized recommendations and insights to encourage users to add more payments and improve their scores.

### Financial literacy

- Developed bite-sized educational modules integrated into the user journey, increasing user confidence.
- Created interactive simulation tools showing how specific actions could affect credit scores.
